# Awesome-Vertical-Industry-CRM

## Top Vertical Industry CRM Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Industry-Specific CRM, Workflow Automation & Self-Hosted Solutions*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial vertical CRM platforms** and **open-source projects** that serve specific industries with tailored workflows, data models, and compliance requirements — from healthcare and finance to construction and nonprofit sectors.



**Examples** include Salesforce Industries Cloud, Veeva Systems, Guidewire, Insurity, nCino, Amdocs, Procore, Toast POS, Blackbaud, and ServiceTitan (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Vertical industry CRM is a growing open-source domain. **CiviCRM** leads as the de facto nonprofit CRM with 20 years of development and thousands of organizations worldwide . **Dolibarr** provides modular ERP/CRM for SMEs across sectors with specialized modules for foundations, point-of-sale, and manufacturing . **SuiteCRM** offers industry-specific customization for construction bidding, subcontractor management, and project tracking . **Krayin** delivers open-source CRM for automotive, hospitality, and banking with omni-channel communication . This section is heavily expanded.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



### Nonprofit & Civic Sector



- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  

  **The leading open-source CRM for nonprofits and mission-driven organizations**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **20 years of development**  . **Contact management, donor lifecycle management, memberships, events, and bulk communications** — built by nonprofit professionals since 2005  . **Used by thousands of organizations worldwide** — no gimmicks or pricing schemes, just CiviCRM  . **SearchKit for no-code data management, FormBuilder and Mosaico for drag-and-drop builders**  . **CiviCRM Standalone** — deploy without CMS overhead  . **Best for nonprofit CRM** .



- **[NowCRM](https://github.com/Nowtec-Solutions/NowCRM)**  

  **Open-source CRM for NGOs**, AGPLv3 licensed  . **Developed during the Ukraine crisis to manage refugee housing** — built to empower organizations with control over their data  . **Self-hosted for maximum data control** or managed SaaS in Switzerland  . **Best for NGOs and humanitarian organizations** .



### Healthcare & Life Sciences



- **[CRMSalud](https://github.com/danjohn007/CRMSalud)**  

  **CRM online specialized for the healthcare sector**, open-source  . **Client management, product catalog, sales pipeline, reports and analytics**  . **Role-based access**: Administrator, Vendedor (Seller), Marketing, Inventarios (Inventory)  . **PHP/MySQL-based with MVC architecture**  . **Best for healthcare clinics and medical practices** .



- **[Linka_AI](https://github.com/GirgesAdam/Linka_AI)**  

  **Clinic management and CRM platform with deterministic booking engine**, open-source  . **Availability calculated from clinic state** — opening hours, doctor schedules, service compatibility, device availability  . **Laser device scheduling with pricing per device** (Prime Lase, Candela Gentle)  . **Patient CRM with notes, tags, leads, tasks, cohorts, campaigns, and conversion attribution**  . **Deterministic analytics** generated by backend services over canonical records, not LLM calculations  . **Best for medical spas and aesthetic clinics** .



### Legal



- **[LexManager](https://github.com/felCardoso/LexManager)**  

  **CRM for law offices built with Flask, SQLAlchemy, and Bootstrap 5**, MIT licensed  . **Client management, process control, Datajud API integration, dashboard with statistics, authentication system, and hearing control**  . **Python 3.8+ with Flask**  . **Best for small law firms** .



- **[crm-juridico](https://github.com/GEDEON1708/crm-juridico)**  

  **Complete management system for law offices**, open-source  . **Clients, cases, appointments, deadlines, documents, and fees management**  . **JWT authentication with refresh tokens, RBAC, Bcrypt password hashing, rate limiting, and Helmet security headers**  . **Full API with endpoints for clients, cases, appointments, deadlines, documents, and fees**  . **Best for law firms needing robust case management** .



### Real Estate



- **[RealEstateCRM](https://github.com/prolinkinfo/RealEstateCRM)**  

  **CRM exclusively designed for real estate agents**, MIT licensed with **21+ GitHub stars**  . **MERN stack** (MongoDB, Express, ReactJS, Node.js) for high performance  . **Intuitive dashboard, responsive design, and integrated communication tools**  . **Best for real estate professionals** .



- **[open-house-crm](https://github.com/serjykalstryke/open-house-crm)**  

  **Open-source CRM platform for real estate professionals**, GPLv3 licensed  . **Contact management, business analytics from transaction history, transaction and inventory databases, and social forum** for vendor contacts and listings  . **Progressive web application tools**  . **Best for real estate agents wanting a developer-friendly CRM** .



### Construction



- **[SuiteCRM for Construction](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  

  **Open-source CRM customizable for construction bidding and client management**, AGPL-3.0 licensed  . **Bid & opportunity pipeline with construction-specific stages**: Lead Identified → Pre-Qualification → Invitation to Bid Received → Estimating → Bid Submitted → Shortlisted → Negotiation → Contract Awarded  . **Subcontractor management with trade specialty, license tracking, insurance certificates, safety record, and quality ratings**  . **Client & developer management with project history, payment reliability, and referral sources**  . **Project tracking with milestones, permits, and inspection status**  . **Contract & change order management with approval workflows**  . **Best for construction contractors and developers** .



- **[OpenConstructionERP](https://github.com/datadrivenconstruction/OpenConstructionERP)**  

  **Open-source construction ERP with CRM capabilities**, open-source  . **Coordination Hub** — single dashboard fusing clashes, RFIs, submittals, and action items per model federation  . **AI Triage** — LLM ranks new clashes by severity, rework cost, and location criticality  . **Tendering** — create bid packages, distribute to subcontractors, collect and compare bids  . **Change orders** with cost and schedule impact analysis  . **HSE incidents** with OSHA-recordable flag and mandatory root-cause fields  . **Best for construction project management with CRM** .



### Insurance



- **[InsurancePro CRM](https://github.com/prolinkinfo/InsuranceProCRM)**  

  **CRM empowering insurance agents to manage clients, policies, and leads**, JavaScript-based with **21 GitHub stars**  . **Comprehensive and intuitive platform** for insurance professionals  . **Best for insurance agents** .



### Travel



- **[CRM for Travel Agency](https://github.com/LucasRibasCardoso/crm-gerenciamento-de-clientes)**  

  **Full-stack CRM designed for a travel agency**, open-source  . **Client management with personal and passport data**  . **Dashboard with metrics** — total clients, new clients in last 30 days, passport status  . **JWT authentication with role-based access control** (ADMIN, MANAGER, USER)  . **Docker Compose deployment** with Clean Architecture backend  . **Best for travel agencies** .



### Education



- **[EduNett](https://github.com/EduNetGroup/EduNett)**  

  **CRM system tailored for learning centers**, .NET framework  . **Streamlines administrative tasks and elevates student engagement**  . **Intuitive student management functionalities and efficient communication tools**  . **Best for learning centers and tutoring businesses** .



- **[TWM (Technology Workplace Manager)](https://github.com/)** — Modular enterprise management with HR, attendance, payroll, and logistics modules  .



### General-Purpose CRM with Vertical Modules



- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  **Open-source ERP/CRM with modular design**, GPL-3.0 licensed  . **Activate only the features you need** — CRM, products, orders, invoices, projects, point-of-sale, foundations member management, donations, and more  . **Country-specific tax support**: Spanish RE/IRPF, French DOM-TOME VAT, Basque TicketBAI, Canadian double taxes, Indian GST, Saudi ZATCA  . **Ready for electronic billing** (FacturX, Peppol)  . **DoliWamp, DoliDeb, DoliRpm auto-installers** for simple setup  . **Best for SMEs wanting modular ERP/CRM** .



- **[Krayin](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)**  

  **Open-source CRM designed for wide range of industries** — automotive, hospitality, banking, and more  . **Omni-channel communication** via email, live chat, social media, and VoIP  . **Data import/export, deduplication, and synchronization** for system interoperability  . **Task and sales process automation**  . **Best for multi-industry CRM** .



- **[Cordys CRM](https://github.com/1Panel-dev/CordysCRM)**  

  **New-generation open-source AI CRM from FIT2CLOUD**, GPL v3 licensed  . **Full L2C (Lead to Cash) management** — lead acquisition, intelligent assignment, customer management, opportunity tracking, contract signing, and payment execution  . **Integrated with DataEase and SQLBot for visual analytics and natural language querying**  . **MaxKB enterprise agent platform** for intelligent creation, follow-up, and quoting  . **Private deployment with data sovereignty**  . **Best for Chinese enterprises and teams wanting AI-native CRM** .

**Frameworks for building custom vertical CRM solutions**: Combine **CiviCRM** for nonprofit and civic sector CRM  . Use **Dolibarr** for modular ERP/CRM across industries  . Deploy **SuiteCRM** for construction and field service customization  . Choose **Krayin** for automotive, hospitality, or banking CRM  . Integrate **CRMSalud** or **Linka_AI** for healthcare  . Use **RealEstateCRM** or **open-house-crm** for real estate  . Choose **InsurancePro CRM** for insurance  . Note that true enterprise vertical CRM with managed infrastructure, industry-specific compliance certifications, and vendor-supported SLAs (Veeva, Guidewire, nCino) remain primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong industry-tailored CRM foundations that require integration for complete vertical deployments.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Vertical CRM platforms handle sensitive client data and may involve industry-specific compliance obligations. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and compliance with data privacy regulations (HIPAA for healthcare, GLBA for finance, etc.).

- **Industry compliance varies significantly** — healthcare CRM must comply with HIPAA, insurance with state regulations, and financial services with GLBA and SOX. Verify compliance before deployment.

- **Open-source vertical CRM projects vary significantly in maturity** — CiviCRM and Dolibarr are production-grade  ; some industry-specific projects are early-stage or proof-of-concept  . Evaluate before relying on them for critical workflows.

- **License considerations**: CiviCRM uses AGPL-3.0  , Dolibarr uses GPL-3.0  , SuiteCRM uses AGPL-3.0  , and Krayin uses MIT  . Verify licensing against your use case before committing.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong industry-tailored CRM foundations, but **managed infrastructure, industry-specific compliance certifications, and vendor-supported SLAs** remain primarily commercial offerings.
